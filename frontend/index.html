Why not send everything to an LLM?
Each stage shrinks the work before the costly one.
Step 1Candidate generation

Applicant × product pairs form the search space.

Step 2SQL pre-filter

Income, credit, age and employment rules drop invalid pairs.

Step 3Deterministic scoring

A transparent weighted score ranks what is left.

Step 4Optional AI

LLM only for qualitative checks that rules cannot answer.
